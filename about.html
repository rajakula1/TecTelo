Why Tectelo Consulting Was Founded

ENGINEERING STANDARDS

The Tectelo Production AI Standard

We adhere to strict engineering principles that distinguish reliable production systems from experimental prototypes.

1. Determinism Where It Counts

LLMs generate natural language, but business APIs require deterministic contracts. We enforce strict JSON schema validation, Pydantic data models, and programmatic guardrails around every agent action.

2. Sub-Second Real-Time Budgets

Conversational Voice AI cannot tolerate 2-second turn pauses. We engineer streaming audio pipelines with WebSockets/WebRTC, VAD interruption gates, and low-latency TTS targeting sub-650ms round-trips.

3. Verbatim Grounding & Zero Hallucination

In healthcare and enterprise knowledge retrieval, hallucination is unacceptable. We design hybrid dense/sparse vector retrieval with cross-encoder rerankers and exact paragraph-level anchor citations.
