COMPLIANCE & DATA GOVERNANCE POLICY

Strict PHI & BAA Engagement Protocol

We enforce strict security boundaries from the very first discovery call to protect your organization and patients:

  • Zero Unprotected PHI: No real Protected Health Information (PHI) is accepted or reviewed during discovery calls or initial assessments without an executed Business Associate Agreement (BAA).
  • Non-PHI by Default: Architecture assessments and rapid prototypes operate strictly on system schemas, data flow diagrams, and synthetic datasets.
  • Scoped BAA Engagements: When an implementation requires live PHI access, it is governed under a dedicated SOW with a signed BAA, isolated cloud environments, and explicit access controls.
  • Clean-Room Deliverables: All consulting deliverables are engineered independently under Tectelo Consulting with clear IP assignment scoped to your Statement of Work.
